📖 HOW TO PLAY
01 GAME FLOW

Each match consists of 4 rounds. Teams alternate between attacking and defending roles every round.

15s Defense Phase Defenders draw walls
→
30s Attack Phase Attackers breach target
02 DEFENDING
🛡️ Draw ink walls anywhere except the protected zone around the target
● Permanent Ink — Stays until destroyed
◐ Fading Ink — Costs 50% less, fades after 8 seconds
03 ATTACKING
⚔️ Draw a continuous line from your spawn zone to the golden target
🎯 Reach the target center to score 100 points
📏 Partial progress awards points based on distance reached
04 COLLISIONS
💥 Hitting enemy ink = 2 second stun + teleport to spawn
✨ Your collision creates an explosion that destroys nearby enemy walls!
05 INK MANAGEMENT
📉 Drains while drawing
📈 Regenerates when idle

Balance aggressive moves with ink conservation for strategic advantage!

💡 PRO TIPS
Coordinate with your teammate to create overlapping defense zones
Use fading ink for quick patches during defense
Attackers: sacrifice yourself strategically to blow holes in walls!
